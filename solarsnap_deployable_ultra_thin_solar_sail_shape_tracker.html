Electro-Mechanical Coupled Physics & Inversion Formulations

Quantitative mathematical framework governing 2.5 µm aluminized polyimide membranes under combined photon radiation pressure, thermal gradients, and coplanar capacitance perturbations.

1. Non-Linear Membrane Wrinkle Mechanics (Föppl–von Kármán)

Ultra-thin gossamer sails cannot sustain compressive stresses ($\sigma_{\min} < 0$). Under unequal perimeter corner loads $F_i$, localized buckling manifests as directional wrinkles:

$$D \nabla^4 w - h \left( \frac{\partial^2 \Phi}{\partial y^2}\frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\partial^2 \Phi}{\partial x^2}\frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial y^2} - 2\frac{\partial^2 \Phi}{\partial x \partial y}\frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial x \partial y} \right) = P_{\text{rad}}(x,y)$$

Where $D = \frac{E h^3}{12(1-\nu^2)}$ represents bending stiffness for membrane thickness $h \approx 2.5\,\mu\text{m}$, $\Phi$ is the Airy stress function, and $w(x,y)$ is normal surface out-of-plane deflection.

Boundary: Corner lanyard tension $F = \oint_{\partial \Omega} \boldsymbol{\sigma} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{n}} \, ds$

2. Conformal Interdigital Grid Trace Capacitance

Printed conductive carbon-nanotube traces along the Kapton substrate generate conformal fringing capacitance across sub-millimeter electrode fingers:

$$C(F, w) = \varepsilon_0 \left( \frac{\varepsilon_{\text{polyimide}} + 1}{2} \right) \frac{K(k)}{K'(k)} L_{\text{trace}} \cdot \left[ 1 - \alpha \left(\frac{\partial w}{\partial s}\right)^2 + \beta \frac{F}{E h} \right]$$

$K(k)$ is the complete elliptic integral of the first kind with modulus $k = \sin\left(\frac{\pi W}{2(W+G)}\right)$. When wrinkles or billows form, out-of-plane slope $\frac{\partial w}{\partial s}$ sharply attenuates fringing flux lines, causing an instantaneous drop in $C$.

Sensitivity: $\Delta C / \Delta w_{\text{rms}} \approx -1.84\,\text{pF/mm}$

3. Kalman State Inversion & Billow Discriminator

The on-board flight computer decouples thermal expansion from mechanical wrinkling via a real-time Jacobin state observer operating on the measured pair $(F, C)$:

$$\begin{bmatrix} \delta w_{\text{billow}} \\ \delta \varepsilon_{\text{strain}} \end{bmatrix} = \mathbf{J}^{-1} \begin{bmatrix} \Delta F - \kappa_T \Delta T \\ \Delta C - \gamma_T \Delta T \end{bmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{J} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\partial F}{\partial w} & \frac{\partial F}{\partial \varepsilon} \\ \frac{\partial C}{\partial w} & \frac{\partial C}{\partial \varepsilon} \end{bmatrix}$$

A high boom tension with low capacitance uniquely signifies symmetric billowing under photon pressure, while asymmetric tension with localized $C$ spikes signals shear rip-lines.

Discriminant: $\det(\mathbf{J}) \neq 0$ across all operational flight regimes

4. Solar Pressure Momentum Transfer & Closed-Loop Trim

Total photon thrust vector $\mathbf{F}_{\text{photon}}$ integrates surface specular ($r_s$) and diffuse ($r_d$) reflection over deformed surface normal vectors $\hat{\mathbf{n}}$:

$$\mathbf{F}_{\text{photon}} = \frac{I_0 A_{\text{sail}}}{c} \iint_{\Omega} \left[ (1 + r_s) (\hat{\mathbf{s}} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{n}})^2 \hat{\mathbf{n}} + (1 - r_s) (\hat{\mathbf{s}} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{n}}) \hat{\mathbf{s}} + \frac{2}{3} r_d (\hat{\mathbf{s}} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{n}}) \hat{\mathbf{n}} \right] d\Omega$$

Whenever $(F, C)$ detects a curvature distortion exceeding $w_{\text{rms}} > 12\,\text{mm}$, motorized spool servos reel perimeter lanyards at rate $\dot{L}_i = -K_p (C_{\text{target}} - C) - K_d \dot{C}$.

Actuation: 4-Corner Brushless Stepper Spools (0.05 mm precision)
